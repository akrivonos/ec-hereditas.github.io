About this website

This website is operated by the European Center for Cultural Heritage Studies Hereditas , a Ukrainian non-governmental organization, and is used to present the organization's activities, research resources and digital projects.

Data collected by this website

This website does not use advertising trackers. Google Analytics is used only after your explicit consent in order to obtain aggregated statistics about visits and use of the website.

Google Analytics code is not loaded before consent has been given. Your analytics preference and your choice of light or dark theme are stored in your browser.
