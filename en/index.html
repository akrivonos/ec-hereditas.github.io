About us

An independent platform for heritage, research and public benefit.

Non-governmental Organization “European Centre for Cultural Heritage Studies Hereditas” was established in Ukraine to preserve, study and share knowledge about tangible and intangible cultural heritage.

We combine humanities research, archival work, field documentation, digital methods and publishing. Our priority is to produce resources that can be used by researchers, educators, cultural institutions and interested communities.

What we do

From preserving a source to making knowledge usable.

01

Digitisation and preservation

Preparation and digital capture of manuscripts, photographs, audio, video and field materials; metadata, backup storage and planning for long-term access.

02

Research and fieldwork

Archival and interdisciplinary research, expeditions, interviews and documentation of local traditions, with attention to ethics and participants’ rights.

03

Digital catalogues and data

Structured multilingual catalogues, controlled terminology, link verification, stable identifiers and traceable data provenance.

04

Publishing and knowledge transfer

Academic and public-facing publications, edited volumes, educational materials, open resources and professional exchange.

Flagship digital project

Hereditas Source Catalogue

A research catalogue of sources and collections concerning folklore, folk beliefs, ritual culture, traditional knowledge and adjacent fields across Europe and the Caucasus. It describes resources — books, manuscripts, archival files, museum objects, audio recordings, field materials and digital collections — rather than presenting unverified practices as fact.

Principles

Quality, responsibility and trust are part of the infrastructure.

Evidence and provenance

We distinguish source data, working assumptions and verified conclusions, retaining the ability to trace each record’s origin.

Responsible access

Openness applies where rights, participant consent, community interests and the nature of sensitive material allow it.

Community participation

Tradition bearers and local communities should be partners in establishing context and conditions of representation, not merely subjects of description.

Interoperable data

We aim for multilingual metadata, persistent identifiers and formats suitable for exchange with archives and research infrastructures.

Human review

Automation supports discovery and organisation, while disputed matches and interpretive decisions are reviewed by people with relevant expertise.

Long-term value

Projects are planned beyond publication, considering storage, documentation, reuse and maintenance.

Current stage

We develop in stages and do not overstate readiness.

Now

Completing organisational formalities, drafting policies, cleaning catalogue data, verifying sources and designing the public application.

Next

Partner pilots with institutions, small-scale digitisation projects, rights and ethics consultations, and a public catalogue launch.

Long term

A multilingual infrastructure, joint research and educational projects, publications and support for the preservation of at-risk collections.
