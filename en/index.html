About the organization

An independent non-governmental organization for heritage, research and public benefit

European Center for Cultural Heritage Studies Hereditas is a Ukrainian non-governmental organization established to preserve, study and disseminate knowledge about tangible and intangible cultural heritage.

We combine humanities research, work with archives and collections, field documentation, digital methods and publishing.

A dedicated area of Hereditas' work is the development of open digital infrastructure that helps institutions work with cultural collections without losing control over their own data, provenance or access rules.

Hereditas Digital Infrastructure

From discovering a source to working with a real collection.

Hereditas is developing an interconnected set of open tools for researching, describing, digitising, migrating and integrating cultural collections. Each product has a distinct role while sharing common principles for data, provenance, access and portability.

01

Hereditas Platform

A multilingual research and discovery catalogue of sources and collections relating to folklore, traditional culture and adjacent areas of cultural heritage across Europe and the Caucasus.

Platform combines normalized entities, exact source forms, provenance, verification levels and conservative deduplication.

Active dataset Public interface in development

Explore Hereditas Platform →

02

Hereditas Collections

An open institutional system for archival, ethnographic, folklore, oral-history and fieldwork collections.

It combines archival hierarchy with the context of expeditions, sessions, people, communities and places, together with physical inventory, digital representations, rights, consent and access policies.

In development Seeking institutional pilot

03

Hereditas Studio

A local-first tool for practical digitisation based on the principle catalogue as you digitise.

Object identification, file sequencing, checksums, technical metadata, derivatives, quality control and OCR / HTR / STT integration are handled within one controlled workflow.

In development Seeking digitisation pilot

04

Hereditas Bridge

A toolkit for migrating, normalizing, validating and integrating data between spreadsheets, legacy systems, Hereditas and external cultural-heritage platforms.

Its principle is simple: data should be able to enter Hereditas and leave it again without hidden loss or vendor lock-in.

In development Seeking migration pilot

Hereditas Core is not a separate user-facing product. It is the shared architectural foundation of Platform, Collections, Studio and Bridge: identifiers, data models, provenance, review workflows, rights, consent, access rules, APIs and data portability.

Hereditas Platform

Catalogue of Sources Across Europe and the Caucasus

Platform helps researchers discover and compare cultural-heritage sources dispersed across archives, libraries, museums, universities, digital repositories, publications and research projects.

The catalogue covers books, manuscripts, archival records, audio and video recordings, field materials, digital collections, bibliographies and research reports.

Its geographic model covers Europe and the Caucasus and takes account not only of present-day states, but also regions, historical-cultural areas and transborder traditions.

For institutions

Technology should serve the collection, not the other way around.

Institutional control

An archive, museum, library or university remains the source of truth for its own collection and retains its local identifiers and working rules.

No forced centralization

Participation in shared discovery should not require an institution to transfer control over originals or its complete information system to Hereditas.

Migration without lock-in

Data should remain importable, exportable and movable between systems without hidden vendor dependency.

Traceable provenance

Original values, sources, transformations and important curatorial decisions should remain reconstructable.

Ethical access

Copyright, privacy, consent, ethical restrictions and cultural protocols should not be reduced to a single “open / closed” field.

Open standards

The infrastructure is designed to interact with other archival, museum and research systems rather than become an isolated database.

Principles

Quality, responsibility and trust are part of the infrastructure.

Evidence and provenance

We distinguish source data, working assumptions and verified conclusions while preserving the ability to reconstruct where a record came from.

Ethical access

Openness applies where rights, participant consent, community interests and the nature of sensitive material allow it.

Community participation

Tradition bearers and local communities should not merely be objects of description, but partners in defining context and conditions of representation.

Interoperability and portability

Open standards, documented structures and complete exports should allow institutions to use Hereditas without losing control over their own data.

Human review

Automation can help discover, transcribe and organize material, but ambiguous matches and substantive decisions should remain open to human review.

Long-term value

Projects are planned beyond the moment of publication, taking preservation, documentation, reuse, migration and maintenance into account.

Institutional pilots

Testing infrastructure against real collections and real working practices.

Hereditas is looking for institutions willing to test small, practically bounded scenarios together with us. A pilot does not require migration of an entire collection or replacement of an institution's current information system.

A

Collections Pilot

A small real collection for testing fonds description, people, places, fieldwork context, physical inventory, digital representations and access rules.

B

Digitisation Pilot

A limited set of materials for testing Studio: object identification, file creation, checksums, technical metadata, quality control and controlled transfer of results.

C

Migration Pilot

XLSX, CSV or a legacy-system export for testing Bridge: profiling, mapping, validation, entity reconciliation, preview and portability without hidden data loss.

D

Methodological Partnership

Collaboration with archivists, ethnographers, folklorists, digital-humanities specialists and experts in rights, ethics, metadata standards, preservation and interoperability.

Publication or federation of a partner collection's metadata through Hereditas Platform is a separate decision. Participation in a Collections, Studio or Bridge pilot does not automatically mean publication of institutional data.

Current stage
