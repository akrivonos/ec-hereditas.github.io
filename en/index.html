About the organization

Research, preservation and digital work with cultural heritage

European Center for Cultural Heritage Studies Hereditas is a Ukrainian non-governmental organization established to research, document and preserve cultural heritage

We work with archival and field materials, digital collections, publications and tools that can support institutions and researchers
