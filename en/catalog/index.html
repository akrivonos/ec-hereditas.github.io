Planned catalogue features

  • combined search across the canonical catalogue, integration queue and research reports;
  • filters by country, region, language, institution, creator, subject, access type and verification level;
  • stable identifiers, source forms and provenance for normalised relationships;
  • clear separation of a digital platform, holding institution, individual resource and digital representation;
  • marking of restricted, sensitive or legally uncertain material.

Current data status

The pilot database contains 3,558 positions: 1,792 canonical resources, 1,640 integration-queue records and 126 full-text reports. It covers 48 countries and territories, 745 regions and 72 languages. These figures describe distinct data layers and do not imply that every candidate has already been verified.

For prospective partners

We are interested in pilots with archives, libraries, museums, universities and community initiatives. Advice on metadata, rights, sensitive collections, multilingual terminology and interoperability with other cultural platforms is particularly welcome.
