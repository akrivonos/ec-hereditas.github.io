What is Hereditas Platform?

Hereditas Platform is a research and discovery catalogue of cultural-heritage sources across Europe and the Caucasus. Its purpose is to help researchers discover, compare and verify materials currently dispersed across libraries, archives, museums, universities, digital repositories, publications and independent research projects.

Platform primarily describes sources, collections and resources rather than presenting individual traditions or practices as unverified facts. A resource can be connected to an institution, region, language, author, collection, digital representation and other related sources.

What the catalogue contains

  • books, articles, edited volumes and serial publications;
  • manuscripts and archival records;
  • archival, library and museum collections;
  • audio and video recordings;
  • fieldwork and expedition materials;
  • digital libraries and repositories;
  • bibliographies and institutional catalogues;
  • research datasets;
  • regional and thematic research reports.

Geographic coverage

The catalogue covers Europe and the Caucasus and is not organized exclusively around present-day states. Its geographic model can separately represent countries, historical and cultural areas, administrative regions, local places and transborder traditions.

This is particularly important for folklore and ethnographic materials, because cultural phenomena, languages and historical communities frequently do not correspond neatly to contemporary political borders.

How the data are organized

Hereditas Platform deliberately distinguishes data with different levels of readiness. A verified record is not treated as equivalent to a candidate still requiring manual review.

Canonical catalogue

Verified and normalized resources with stable identifiers. Records may contain linked authors, institutions, countries, regions, languages, genres, access states, verification levels, URLs and source notes.

Integration queue

Candidates originating from regional spreadsheets, imports and other research sources. A candidate may turn out to be a new resource, a possible duplicate, a child object or a record for which there is not yet enough evidence for a final decision.

Research reports

Full texts of regional and thematic research reports are retained for full-text search, contextual verification and reconstruction of the provenance of structured records.

Principles of data management

  • Provenance is preserved. It should remain possible to determine where a record, relationship or normalized value originated.
  • Source forms are not destroyed. Original names, spellings and terms are preserved alongside normalized forms.
  • Ambiguous matches are not merged automatically. A similar title or URL may create a candidate for review, but does not by itself justify a merge.
  • Different entities remain distinct. An institution, digital platform, individual resource, author and digital representation are represented separately.
  • Uncertainty remains visible. A working hypothesis should not appear as a confirmed archival fact.
  • Automation does not replace review. Machine-assisted methods can help reconcile and structure material, but ambiguous decisions remain subject to human review.
  • Access is not assumed to be open. Rights, ethical restrictions and material sensitivity must be considered separately.

Current state of Platform

As of 31 July 2026, the working Hereditas Platform dataset contains 3,558 entries: 1,792 canonical resources, 1,640 integration-queue records and 126 full-text research reports.

Normalized authorities cover 48 countries and territories, 745 regions and 72 languages.

These figures describe different layers of the system. Integration-queue records are not automatically considered verified, new or distinct resources.

Next stage

Current work focuses on data cleanup, source and URL verification, development of normalized authorities, improvement of multilingual discovery and preparation of the public interface.

In the longer-term architecture, Platform is also intended to support discovery across partner institutions' collections while preserving their local identifiers, provenance, access rules and status as the authoritative source for their own records.

Hereditas Platform and institutional pilots

Development of Platform forms part of the broader Hereditas digital infrastructure. Other tools in this ecosystem are designed to work directly inside archives, museums, libraries and research collections.

The first institutional pilots are planned primarily for:

  • Hereditas Collections — collection description and management;
  • Hereditas Studio — practical digitisation workflows;
  • Hereditas Bridge — migration, normalization and integration of existing institutional data.

Selected metadata from such collections may later become discoverable through Hereditas Platform where the institution chooses this and where legal and ethical access conditions permit it.

For researchers and institutions

We welcome collaboration with archives, libraries, museums, universities, researchers and holders of digital collections in developing and reviewing Platform.

Particularly useful contributions include:

  • information about missing catalogues, collections, bibliographies and digital resources;
  • corrections to institutional names and attributions;
  • identification of outdated or incorrect URLs;
  • advice on regional, historical and multilingual terminology;
  • academic review of regional or thematic sections;
  • discussion of future federation of institutional catalogues and collections.
